After Bilawal Sidhu's 4D deer: every frame of a fixed-camera clip, cut out by SAM 3.1 and lifted into 3D with Depth Anything V2, so the animal is left where and when it actually was. Source: @Trail_Cams, 80 tracked frames. The top bar drives every panel's clock; drag panels to orbit, scroll inside a panel where it says so.

01 · MillipedeThe reference effect. Each frame's bear stays where it stood; the recording camera's frustum and its live frame sweep the trail.
02 · Scroll follows the motionDetects whether the subject comes toward or goes away from the lens, then scrolling here flies you along its real direction of travel. Scroll inside.
03 · Rotate time into spaceA rotation in the x–t plane of the video volume. 0° is the ordinary picture; 90° turns the time axis sideways, so still things streak and the bear carves a path. Scroll inside · click to swap x/y.
04 · Space-time blockFull-frame cut-outs stacked along t in their true positions, the empty river behind. The present slice is lit.
05 · WorldlineA spacetime diagram: the ground (x, depth) is the floor, time is up. The bear's path is a tube, the present is the glowing plane slicing it.
06 · Colour is timeEvery lifted point coloured by the moment it was recorded, violet to red. The current instant flashes white as it passes through.
07 · Slit-scanTwo cuts through the video cube: the column under the line over time (right), the row under the line over time (below). Move over the picture.
08 · ChronophotographMarey and Muybridge: the empty river with every sixth pose laid in, older poses fainter, and the poses as a strip.
09 · EchoThe footage playing with the last two seconds of the bear trailing behind it — the flat, real-time version of all of the above.
