Three renderings of one trail-cam clip, built from the technical spec. The top bar drives all three clocks; drag a panel to orbit, scroll to zoom.

1 · 4D volumetric point-cloud trailEvery frame's bear is lifted into one fixed world and merged into a single vertex buffer (~15% density). A shader compares each point's a_frameId to the playhead: points within Δt glow cyan at α 1.0, the rest sit at α 0.08. The wireframe pyramid is the recording camera, its quad the live frame.
2 · 3.5D segmented chrono-extrusionCut-out + depth for the last 64 frames live in a texture-array ring buffer (one layer written per frame, nothing shifted). 64 instanced 256×144 grids are spaced along a direction vector, relief-displaced by depth and faded by α·e−λi; i = 0 is the newest.
3 · Spatiotemporal video volumeThe clip stacked along Z as a 256×144×80 sampler3D and ray-marched (256 steps, α 0.012). The frame being played is a near-opaque cross-section scrubbing through the cube; frames still to come fog the view in front of it.
