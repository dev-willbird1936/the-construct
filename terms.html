Terms & privacy

The short version: your clips stay yours. We process them to build your visualisations, show published builds to other signed-in visitors after approval, and delete on request.

What you agree to when you upload

  • You have the right to use the clip — you filmed it, or you have permission.
  • Frames of your clip are sent to Meta’s Model API (SAM 3.1 for picking and tracking, Muse Image for background plates, Muse Spark for routing) in standard mode — not used for training.
  • Published builds are shown to other signed-in users inside their tesseract, after owner approval. Nothing unpublished leaves your account.

What is stored

  • Your account (email, sign-in records) and your uploads (the clip, one still per picked frame).
  • Your builds (cut-outs, plates, viewer files) and their status (queued, done, published, reported).
  • A cost ledger (how many model calls your builds used) and reports filed against builds.

Retention

Uploads are deleted after 30 days unless they back a published build. Unpublished builds and their uploads can be deleted at any time from My builds. Published builds stay until you unpublish or delete them.

How to delete

Use the Delete button in My builds next to the upload step. It removes the build, its files and its upload. If the button ever fails, file a Report on the build with the words “delete request” and the owner removes it by hand.

Reports and approval

Every published build passes the owner before it reaches other visitors’ tesseracts. Any signed-in visitor can report a build; reported builds are hidden while the owner looks at them.

Who to contact

Write to the operator at the address in your sign-in email for access, correction, deletion, or any question about your clips and builds.
